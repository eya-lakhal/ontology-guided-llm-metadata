UTI Check: decision support for suspected urinary tract infection

UTI Check is a web application released in 2024 by the (fictional) CareHome Stewardship Lab.
Nurses answer a short series of questions at the bedside (fever, new urinary symptoms, delirium, catheter) and the tool tells them whether a urine test is useful and whether the GP should be called. A summary is generated for the physician.
It aims to improve diagnosis and to avoid unnecessary antibiotics for asymptomatic bacteriuria, while supporting appropriate prescribing when an infection is likely.
Free to use; intended for professionals only, not for residents or families.
