Hand rub in 7 steps

A one-page practical sheet published in 2022 by the (fictional) Northfield Infection Prevention Network.
It shows care assistants and nurses the seven steps of alcohol-based hand rub, when to perform it (before and after resident contact, after removing gloves) and common mistakes such as wearing rings or watches.
The aim is to prevent infections and the spread of resistant bacteria between residents.
