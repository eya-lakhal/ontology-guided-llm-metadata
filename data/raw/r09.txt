Antimicrobial resistance in long-term care: an open online course

This MOOC, launched in 2025 by the (fictional) European Long-Term Care Academy, is open to all staff working in nursing homes: nurses, physicians, pharmacists and care assistants.
Over four weeks it explains how antimicrobial resistance emerges, why nursing homes are reservoirs of multidrug-resistant bacteria, and what every professional can do.
Its objectives are to raise awareness and to train staff. The course is free and its materials are released under a Creative Commons licence.
