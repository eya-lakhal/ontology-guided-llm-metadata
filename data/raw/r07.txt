Flu season: protect yourself, protect your residents

A poster published in 2022 by the (fictional) Riverside Care Homes Group.
It encourages nurses and care assistants to get the flu vaccine every autumn and invites residents to check their pneumococcal vaccination with their doctor.
The poster explains that influenza and pneumonia are frequent causes of hospitalisation and of antibiotic use in nursing homes.
Its goal is to raise awareness and prevent infections. Printed A3 poster for staff rooms and corridors.
